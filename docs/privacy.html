Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

QuickMD does not collect, store or send any personal data.

  • Your notes stay on your Mac, in the files and folders you choose.
  • There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. The network permission is only there because Apple's WebKit, used for printing and PDF export, needs it to start.
  • Links you ⌘-click open in your own browser; QuickMD doesn't see what happens there.

Questions: see the support page.
