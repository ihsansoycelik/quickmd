# Code of Conduct

QuickMD follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different views and experience.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or private.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report problems privately through GitHub: open a [private report](https://github.com/ihsansoycelik/quickmd/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct". Only the maintainer sees it. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban a contributor, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
